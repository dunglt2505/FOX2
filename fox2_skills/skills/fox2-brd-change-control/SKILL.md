# Assess a bounded BRD change before canonical publication

- Read the supplied requirement baseline and proposed delta. Preserve their actual status and provenance, including Candidate or conditional material.
- Trace each change to affected acceptance criteria, interfaces, consumers and implementation/tests. Separate business-semantic changes from machine-identity-only updates.
- Deliver a draft impact assessment and the specific owning approvals needed; do not invent approval receipts.
- Continue the assessment under conditional sources. Hold canonical BRD edits, acceptance promotion or release until their governing approval is established.

Read [scope and source guidance](../../shared/host_guidance.md) when authority, canonical sources, task state or effects matter.
