# Update a milestone view from evidence-backed health facts

- Establish cutoff and reuse the existing health report, acceptance results, milestones and plan rather than inventing a competing master status.
- Distinguish completed, in progress, blocked and unknown work from actual evidence. Do not equate a commit or draft delivery with milestone acceptance.
- Produce the requested draft status or roadmap delta, with material dependencies and next milestone. State the provenance of conditional baseline facts.
- Hold authoritative plan publication or release decisions that lack owning approval; continue the factual status analysis.

Read [scope and source guidance](../../shared/host_guidance.md) when authority, canonical sources, task state or effects matter.
