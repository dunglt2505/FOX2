# Evaluate research evidence and its limits

- Inspect the actual method, data lineage/split, labels, feature availability, backtest procedure, costs and applicability claims supplied.
- Check leakage, lookahead, survivorship, overfitting, train/test contamination and unsupported extrapolation. Distinguish a documented method from executed empirical evidence.
- Give a justified evidence tier, concrete defects and a bounded next validation step. Keep missing observations explicit rather than inventing backtest performance.
- Analytical review can proceed under conditional provenance. A research finding does not grant deployment, provider/broker access or LIVE authority.
