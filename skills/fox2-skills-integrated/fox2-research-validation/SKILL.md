# fox2-research-validation

## Agile Lite Owner-approved precedence

Policy v2.1.0 and `../shared/operating_rules.md` govern ordinary internal project work. Use one Kanban task with real output, proportionate checks and CHAT acceptance. Historical R0-R7, PreExec receipts, source-locked Dispatch and extra review apply only when the chosen technical transport or consequential action requires them. Preserve specialized analysis, real permissions, historical evidence and no-replay; do not propagate version changes without material consumer impact.

## Activate when
Research, backtest, strategy claims or feasibility need scrutiny.

## Work to perform
Check source provenance, reproducibility, sample bias, survivorship, data freshness/as-of boundaries, out-of-sample robustness, costs/fees and negative baselines. Label synthetic fixtures; never infer trade readiness from preliminary study.

## Completion / stop
Verified methods/results, limitations, decision for experiment or HOLD; no autonomous LIVE.


Completion is governed by the **Execution Contract v0.3.6** below; apply conditional obligations only when triggered, report bounded HOLD otherwise.

## Execution Contract v0.3.6 — observable completion
- **TRIGGER:** Empirical research, investment/backtest claim, risk or profit-protection methodology evaluation.
- **PRECONDITIONS:** Research question, dataset/source provenance, time scope, empirical evidence and applicability limits.
- **REQUIRED_ACTIONS:** Evaluate bias, sample/out-of-sample design, costs, negative baselines and reproducibility; separate hypothesis from deployment evidence.
- **REQUIRED_EVIDENCE:** Dataset as-of, methods/results, statistical/backtest evidence and missing controls.
- **DELIVERABLES:** Research verdict with cited method, robustness, limitations and experiment/HOLD decision.
- **VALIDATION:** Cross-check results against cited method and data provenance; synthetic fixtures do not count as empirical verification.
- **FAILURE_STATE:** `RESEARCH_NOT_VERIFIED` / `BACKTEST_INSUFFICIENT` / `LIVE_HOLD` for missing proof.
- **COMPLETION_GATE:** Bounded empirical assessment and next experiment; NEVER broker/LIVE authorization from research.
## Evidence-to-product boundary
Select source provenance, datasets, time/as-of semantics and research/acceptance documents using authorized Dictionary; distinguish synthetic fixtures from empirical tests. Any research-to-code task uses a source-bound, Git-published Agent prompt and Git result review; direct CHAT research does not invent Agent artifacts. Owner may waive internal review explicitly but cannot transform unvalidated backtests into live execution proof or brokerage authorization.
